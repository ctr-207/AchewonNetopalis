Beyond the Lodge

Section Events

Achewon Netopalis is part of Section E18, a group of lodges across our region that come together twice a year. Both events are open to any Arrowman who wants to go.

SWFT

Section Winter Fellowship & Training

A winter weekend spent indoors with the rest of Section E18. Expect games, bonding, and hanging out with Arrowmen from other lodges, plus a few short workshop sessions where you can learn more about specific OA roles and help plan the upcoming Conclave.

Conclave

Section-Wide Spring Gathering

Basically Scout camp for a weekend. Every lodge in Section E18 comes together each spring for games, competitions, evening shows, and serious patch trading. Lodge delegates also vote on the Section's next youth officers.
