Membership

Unit Elections

Each spring, every troop and crew in the council holds an election. Scouts and Scouters cast a quiet vote for the members of their own unit who best exemplify the Scout Oath and Law in their daily lives. Those chosen are welcomed into Achewon Netopalis, not for what they've achieved, but for how they've served.

Eligibility

To be eligible, a Scout must hold a Boy Scouts of America membership, have experienced 15 days and nights of camping within the last two years, and be under 21 years of age. Adult Scouters are also eligible under separate criteria set by the council.

How a Unit Requests an Election

A Scoutmaster or Crew Advisor can request an election through the council. A lodge election team will visit the unit's meeting, explain the Order, and conduct the vote.

After the Election

Newly elected members are invited to complete their Ordeal at the next lodge event, the first step of membership. Six months later, they become eligible for the Brotherhood honor.
